Referral Decision Support Tool

For clinicians, GPs and care coordinators referring in Darwin and the Top End, NT. Follows the age-based referral pathway. Not a diagnosis.

Emergency: call 000. Crisis support: Lifeline 13 11 14 (text 0477 13 11 14), Suicide Call Back Service 1300 659 467, NT Mental Health Line 1800 682 288, Kids Helpline 1800 55 1800, 13YARN 13 92 76. Early psychosis criteria are from the headspace Early Psychosis GP checklist. Support only; never replaces clinical judgment. Service details change, so confirm before referring. Don't enter names or identifying details.
